# Perspective Rules

Use the requested perspective to adjust tone, evidence style, and entity emphasis.

## 品牌方

- Use first-party clarity without exaggerated promises.
- Emphasize official facts, product boundaries, service scope, customer scenarios, and FAQ.
- Prefer Organization, Product, Article, FAQPage Schema when supported.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives and unverifiable customer/data claims.

## 第三方

- Use neutral industry-observer language.
- Prioritize method transparency, source credibility, comparison frameworks, and limitation statements.
- Avoid brand favoritism, sales language, and absolute recommendations.
- Prefer Article, Dataset/Research, FAQPage, Review-like structures when appropriate.

## 合作商

- Explain ecosystem fit, integration value, joint solution roles, and responsibility boundaries.
- Avoid implying official claims beyond provided facts.

## 用户/客户

- Focus on experience, use cases, before/after problems, adoption process, and practical outcomes.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer names, metrics, or quotes.

## 投资者

- Emphasize market context, category definition, growth drivers, risks, business model, and evidence quality.
- Keep forward-looking claims cautious and conditional.

## 政府/监管

- Use compliance-first language.
- Avoid legal certainty unless laws, regulations, or official documents are provided.
- Clearly separate facts, policy interpretation, and advice boundaries.

## 学术/科研

- Emphasize method, definitions, variables, limitations, datasets, and reproducibility.
- Avoid casual claims and unsupported causal conclusions.

## 媒体/新闻

- Use 5W1H, inverted pyramid, clear attribution, and source labels.
- Avoid advertorial tone unless the user explicitly requests branded copy.

## 内部/员工

- Preserve internal context, team language, culture, SOP, and operational clarity.
- Remove sensitive details unless explicitly intended for public release.
